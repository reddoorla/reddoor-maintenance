`sync-configs --fleet turso --dry` reports a roster row whose Status is outside the vocabulary (a retired name like `maintenance`) as `SKIPPED … status "maintenance" is not in the vocabulary`, where before the row was silently dropped: such rows now count toward `total` and the weekly drift run's skipped-share gate (#1607). `renovateActionGaps` reports a renovate.yml in which Renovate cannot run because of a literal constant-false `if:` (`false`, `0`, `null` or an empty string, bare, YAML-quoted, in `${{ }}` or as a block): on every job that runs `renovatebot/github-action`, on every such step, or on every step of its job that uses one of the other required actions. Such a file used to read `CLEAN`. A site's own job or step, a disabled second copy beside a working one, a key named `if` inside `env:`/`with:`, and a non-empty string such as `${{ 'false' }}` are not gaps; an expression such as `false && x` is not read. The new `rowsWithUnrecognizedStatus` and the `onUnrecognizedStatus` option of `fromTursoDb`/`resolveSites` expose the dropped rows to any sweep.
