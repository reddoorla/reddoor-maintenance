FAQ
We’ve gathered a list of inquiries from purchasers over the years. If your question isn’t answered below, please contact us.
Q: Why buy bulk PPE?
Due to shifting costs, delayed shipping timelines, and consistent stock issues, it can be very difficult to secure the products you need, in the timing that you require it. By purchasing in bulk, we can lock in best pricing for the entire order and secure goods stateside in limited shipments, thus reducing risk and guaranteeing your people have the items they need, when they need them. Securing costs, availability, and accessibility of your products removes the variables and frustration that come with being dependent on stock or market forces.
Q: What advantages are realized working with a smaller supply firm?
We are able to provide a superior level of service and attention that the larger suppliers just can’t match. We are our own shareholders, so we answer to ourselves…not to a board room or leadership team who are only looking at limited metrics. Like a speed boat around a tanker, we can make quick moves and remain flexible for the needs of our clients.
Q: Do you have a catalog of products?
Our inventory is curated to meet client needs. As a result of our supply chain and manufacturing relationships, we have access to the full spectrum of products available. Please let us know what types of items you’re looking to secure and we’ll take good care of you.
Q: What types of products are you able to source?
Basically, anything you’ve used in your business to date or even things you dream of adding into the mix. We source all types of protective products and equipment, but specialize in personal protection items such as masks, shields, gowns, covers (foot and hair), sanitizers, and gloves.
Q: Will we pay more for a custom program?
The simple response is NO. In addition to cost savings, you may find the savings of time and reductions of supply chain issues to be the real value. Locking in bulk pricing and securing your goods for on-demand use, means you can avoid price hikes (as we’ve seen throughout 2020 & early 2021), limit the pain of constant sourcing, and reduce risks of upsetting internal processes. Having a dedicated and custom PPE program is not only wise logistically, but makes complete sense for any sized organization looking to streamline their workflow and maximize savings.
Q: What if I also have general merchandise needs (in addition to PPE)?
We have a partner company that is the best in the business for supplying custom branded merchandise for promotion or resale. Black Box Merch has been outfitting companies and non-profit organizations for decades with the best quality products. If it exists or can be dreamed up, and you want your brand on it, Black Box Merch can do it! Anything from custom apparel, to headware, drinkware, signage, display advertising, office items, tools, accessories, and so much more. Check them out today for all of your merch needs!
Q: What is included in the sample kit?
We’ll send you a selection of the most popular items we supply to our current customers. There are several variations and options available, so this kit will serve as an introduction and allow you to see several items first hand as well as gauge the type of quality we provide. If there is a specific item you’d like to see and hold, please let us know and we can provide additional samples at any time, in addition to the initial kit.
Q: Can I speak to someone directly?
Definitely! You will have a dedicated client specialist from day one, so let’s cut through any burdensome process and talk right away to discuss your specific needs. Your specialist will be available by phone, email, or text to you at anytime. Send us a quick message and we’ll get you in touch with your primary contact right away.
Q: What is required to complete my purchase?
Once you and your client specialist have determined the right products, quantities, and corresponding details (level, color, delivery specs, etc.), we’ll provide you with final sales orders and financial requirements. We’ll then ask for a purchase order and you’ll be all set. We like to keep the communication clear and the ordering process super smooth.
Q: Is it possible to select how products are packed and/or delivered?
Yes. While all items come packaged to meet certain logistic requirements, we understand that those parameters don’t always line up with how you need to receive or store certain items. We’ll always disclose how they come stock and please let us know if we need to provide other considerations for you (service fees may apply). As for delivery, we are flexible to meet your needs. We can deliver in bulk to your dock, split shipments to select locations, or deliver specific items directly to your departments. We provide you the flexibility to deliver in the ways that make your job easier and keep your people moving on the important things.
Q: What is a stock and ship program?
Buying in bulk can create space issues for some clients or generate the need to maximize their revenue producing footprint for things other than storage, so we offer a unique stock and ship service. We can provide a scalable storage solution for your products and ship or deliver on a regular rhythm to fit your schedule (storage fees may apply). Need weekly, twice monthly, or monthly deliveries? We can tailor your program to fit your organizational needs and goals. Stock and ship programs typically last between 6 months and 2 years.
Q: Where do the products come from?
We provide products that are produced in several countries around the world. We also proudly supply products made in the USA. Some countries of origin outside of the USA include, but are not limited to, Vietnam, China, Taiwan, and Mexico.
Q: Do you maintain a proper chain of custody?
Always. We keep detailed records of all items we source and supply in order to maintain appropriate standards and verify quality all the way to your dock. Our manufacturers are required to do the same and provide us with transparency, thus maintaining the highest possible standards and allowing us to provide you with the best quality products.
Q: What type of quality control measures have been implemented to maintain
product quality?
Management of overseas manufacturing can be tough, which is why many are forced to depend on the word of large supply houses or take their chances with overseas production. We have on-the-ground quality control checks during production as well as visual and written checks prior to loading containers. In addition, we execute quality verification upon receipt of goods and before taking title of ownership. With specific financial triggers in place and trust built by years of production together, we are able to mitigate risk and maintain quality standards throughout the process.
Q: How long does it take?
This is a common question and always a moving target since it really depends on the products, quantities, location of manufacture, financial requirements, and finalization of the order. That said, we are able to move quickly and make a lot happen, so let’s get your order queued up and we can give you a timeline before you press ‘go’!
