## 2026-10-12 — MSOT's home page passes Lighthouse accessibility again, after two design calls and a pixel test that had to be rebuilt (P1-43, medical-solutions-of-texas#77, `709079f`)

The `fleet-lighthouse` nightly printed
`LIGHTHOUSE_FAILURES assertions=accessibility:0.92<0.95 audits=accessibility/color-contrast:w7:3/3,accessibility/heading-order:w3:3/3 site=msot`
on 10-09, 10-10 and 10-11. MSOT's next Maintenance report is due 11-21 and
carries the score. The work started from a worker brief. Turso's
`site_health.lighthouse_failing_audits` for `msot` said the same, read with a
SELECT-only client and the UPDATE control. The nightly's deployed-mode lhci
config (desktop preset, devtools throttling, 3 runs), run by hand against
production at 02:43Z, scored 0.92, 0.92 and 0.92, with the same four nodes
each time. All four are on `/`:

- two color-contrast nodes: "We'll Get You", and the ghosted "contract
  expertise" in the rotating list;
- two heading-order nodes: an `h5` after an `h2`, and a step number "01" as
  an `h4` after an `h2`.

Fixing only the headings would have scored about 0.944. That is under the
gate, so the contrast nodes had to pass too.

**Both contrast fixes were design calls. The first draft treated them as
tone tweaks.** The brief allowed "a text or background tone inside the
existing palette" and stopped only at a visible brand-colour change. The
first draft raised the ghost words from 25% to 75% opacity, giving 3.30:1,
and changed the video box from `bg-light` to `bg-dark`, giving 6.85:1 in
axe. It then went to a 3-lens review.

- **The ghost words.** The design lens showed that 75% takes an inactive
  word to 1.62:1 from the lit one, down from 3.76:1. The one-word spotlight
  mostly goes.
- **The video box.** axe had measured "We'll Get You" against the box's
  `bg-light` under the `mix-blend-multiply` layer: `#b3a983`, mid times
  light. The placeholder image covers that box in every state, so the
  `bg-dark` change moved the instrument's reading and nothing a visitor
  sees. Against the real pixels, the brightest 5% behind the heading were
  1.9:1 on the still at 1440, and 1.6–2.2:1 behind "YOU" on a production
  frame with the surgical lamp in it.

Both went to the operator as Operator decisions 106, with screenshots of
each option. He chose 75% for the ghosts. For the heading he chose a scrim,
a bottom gradient from black 50% to 0, plus `bg-dark`. He asked for the
cases the nightly does not see to be filed (medical-solutions-of-texas#78,
#79).

**What shipped.** Against the still, 99% of the pixels behind the heading
now reach 4.30:1 at 1350 and 4.65:1 at 390. Without the scrim they reach
1.45:1 and 1.93:1.

- axe now stops at the gradient and reports the heading "incomplete". So
  Lighthouse no longer judges that node, and `bg-dark` matters only if the
  image never paints. The PR body says so.
- At 75%, the cut-off first line of the third word ("DIRECT") became
  prominent at 1024 and 390, so a word two steps from the lit one is
  hidden. Option A as asked had said a clip fix would be needed.
- The headings keep their look through `.h4` and `.h5` classes, which share
  the element rules. A full-page pixel diff confirmed it.

**The second review was dirty, on the test, not the page.** The first pixel
check took the heading colour from CSS and filtered out pixels near it. The
instrument lens showed three faults:

- It would pass with the heading drawn under the overlays. The heading
  rendered at rgb(76, 70, 49), and the check reported 4.43 against a true
  1.13.
- Its 5th percentile was set by anti-aliased glyph edges.
- It never ran at 390.

It now takes two screenshots, one with the text drawn and one with it
transparent. Four mutations turn it red: no scrim, the heading under the
overlays (1.03), a 20% scrim, and the heading hidden. That made two dirty
rounds, so the PR went to the operator as Operator decisions 109, rather
than into a third round. He answered: land with no third round, and file
the case nothing measures. That case is the Vimeo video at 390, where
"WE'LL GET" sits mid-box under about 20% scrim, about 2.4:1 on bright
frames (medical-solutions-of-texas#80).

**Beliefs corrected on contact.**

- **"Lighthouse measured the text against the footage."** It measured
  against a box colour nobody sees, and once the scrim went in it stopped
  measuring that text at all. A score of 1.0 now means that node was not
  judged, not that it passed. The pixel test is the only instrument on it.
- **"The nightly uses axe-core 4.14."** The hand-run used 4.14, from an
  `npx` cache. This repo's lockfile pins `@lhci/cli` 0.15.1, which pulls
  axe-core 4.13.0. The nightly spawns `npx --yes @lhci/cli` from a temp
  directory, so which one it loads is not proven. Both versions reported
  the same four nodes, and the site's spec uses 4.13.
- **Item numbers are not stable before they land.** This item was asked as
  107 and landed as 109. The Monday PM pass took 107 and P1-44 took 108
  while #1294 waited, and each merge from `main` conflicted on
  `docs/BACKLOG.md`. The site's own journal entry for #77 cites 108,
  because it was written between the two renumberings. Issue #80 and
  #77's body were corrected to 109.
