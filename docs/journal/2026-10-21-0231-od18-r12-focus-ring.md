## 2026-10-21 — OD 18 / #674: R12, the focus ring (this PR)

R12 is the fourth rule in the operator's encode order: keyboard focus is always visible. The ring contrasts at least 3:1 with the ground it is drawn on, is not clipped, and is never under a pinned bar. It joins R2 and R9 in `src/audits/design-rules/`, as `focus-ring.ts`. This PR stacks on R9's PR, reddoorla/reddoor-maintenance#1588, because both register in the same files.

**The measure is WCAG 2.4.13's own.** The page is tabbed through at 1440×900 and 390×844, under reduced motion, so a ring that transitions in is there at once. At each stop the focused element's box, padded by 16 px (8 px before review round 1), is captured focused and then blurred. The indicator is the pixels that change, and each is judged against the same pixel unfocused: the ground actually painted under it, whether a photo, a gradient or a band.

A stop fails when:

- no pixel changes (`outline: none`, or a ring the colour of its ground);
- the changed pixels' 90th-percentile contrast against their unfocused selves is under 3:1;
- fewer pixels change than half the element's perimeter, so the ring is mostly cut off;
- something else covers the element's centre, such as a pinned bar.

**Corrected on contact: the first measure counted the wrong thing.** It counted the changed pixels that reached 3:1 each. A 1 px ring at a fractional position is anti-aliased into two half-strength rows, so that count measured the ring's sub-pixel offset, not its colour. vidalegacy.org's footer links failed on it, and a fixture copies them. The ring's contrast is now the 90th percentile over all its changed pixels, and the area test is separate.

**Review round 1 was dirty**, with fixes for each finding:

- **False passes in the tab loop:**
  - it stopped at the first iframe with links, and never reached the stops after it;
  - two links whose truncated paths matched read as a wrap, and ended it;
  - a stop within 8 px of the viewport's edge was skipped, so a full-bleed link with `outline: none` passed;
  - a page where nothing was measured passed.

  Stops are now told apart by element identity. Focus inside a frame is counted and stepped through. A stop is clamped to the viewport and measured on the part the screen shows. Nothing measured is unmeasured.

- **False fails:**
  - a loader overlay, now settled first;
  - a Tailwind `peer sr-only` radio whose ring its label draws, which read as "under its label", and is now measured by its label;
  - an `outline-offset` of 12 px beyond the 8 px capture margin, which is now 16 px.
- **A misleading message.** An underline-only focus style said "clipped". It now says "clipped, or not a ring". Whether an underline alone meets R12 is the operator's to say. The rule text asks for a ring, and the check fails it.
- **Admitted, not built:**
  - a focus that opens something inside the captured box (a menu) counts as the indicator, since a pixel comparison cannot tell it from a ring;
  - two stops with the same path have their findings merged.
- **Minor folded in.** The unused `focusVisible` field is gone.

**Review round 2 was dirty**, with one reproduced false fail. Folded in: this entry's own capture margin, which still said 8 px.

**Not covered:**

- a stop wholly outside the viewport after focus scrolled it, and focus inside a frame (each counted in the note);
- a ring drawn more than 16 px from the box;
- a focus that opens something inside the captured box, such as a menu, which counts as the indicator;
- `outline: none` in forced-colours mode;
- focus moved by script (a trapped dialog is tabbed as the page tabs it);
- a focus style that changes only the element's own fill, which is judged like any other change.
