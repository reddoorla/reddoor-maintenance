## 2026-10-21 — OD 18 / #674: R12, the focus ring (this PR)

R12 is the fourth rule in the operator's encode order: keyboard focus is always visible. The ring contrasts at least 3:1 with the ground it is drawn on, is not clipped, and is never under a pinned bar. It joins R2 and R9 in `src/audits/design-rules/`, as `focus-ring.ts`. This PR stacks on R9's PR, reddoorla/reddoor-maintenance#1588, because both register in the same files.

**The measure is WCAG 2.4.13's own.** The page is tabbed through at 1440×900 and 390×844, under reduced motion, so a ring that transitions in is there at once. At each stop the focused element's box, padded by 8 px, is captured focused and then blurred. The indicator is the pixels that change, and each is judged against the same pixel unfocused: the ground actually painted under it, whether a photo, a gradient or a band.

A stop fails when:

- no pixel changes (`outline: none`, or a ring the colour of its ground);
- the changed pixels' 90th-percentile contrast against their unfocused selves is under 3:1;
- fewer pixels change than half the element's perimeter, so the ring is mostly cut off;
- something else covers the element's centre, such as a pinned bar.

**Corrected on contact: the first measure counted the wrong thing.** It counted the changed pixels that reached 3:1 each. A 1 px ring at a fractional position is anti-aliased into two half-strength rows, so that count measured the ring's sub-pixel offset, not its colour. vidalegacy.org's footer links failed on it, and a fixture copies them. The ring's contrast is now the 90th percentile over all its changed pixels, and the area test is separate.

**Findings on live sites, reported here only.** I filed nothing in those repos.

- **www.beachfrontdentistry.com:** seven rings reach between 2.14:1 and 2.99:1 against their ground.
- **reddoorla.com:**
  - 13 stops sit under a `div.bg-white` panel when focused;
  - 11 show no indicator;
  - four sit under a hero image, and one under a fixed bar's button;
  - one reaches 1.12:1.

**Not covered:**

- a stop outside the viewport after focus scrolled it (counted in the note);
- `outline: none` in forced-colours mode;
- focus moved by script (a trapped dialog is tabbed as the page tabs it);
- a focus style that changes only the element's own fill, which is judged like any other change.

- a faint ring with a small dark mark now fails on its 90th percentile;
