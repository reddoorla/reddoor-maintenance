# 2026-11-23 — Code never upgrades an accuracy verdict to `confirmed` (this PR, `claude/b02-never-upgrade-to-confirmed`)

`verifyQuotes` had one rule that moved a verdict up. When the model called a claim `contradicted` and the site used one of the claim's distinctive search terms exactly, the verdict became `confirmed`, with an 80-character window around the term as the "site quote". It was written for our own site, which lists an out-of-state mailing address on one page and its metro area on another: the model called the metro claim contradicted, and the site does say both.

**What it did to a stored report.** On a prospect audit from 11-04, two claims the model had called contradicted were printed under "checked and fine" as matching a passage on the business's own site. One said the business is a travel-nurse staffing agency; the other said no business operates under its exact name. The terms that did the flipping were the model's evidence *against* the claims: the business's own name in its footer, and "med-surg suppliers", the phrase that says what the business actually is. Finding them on the site was taken as the site agreeing. The window then passed `quoteSupportsClaim` because it shared two words with the claim ("healthcare" and "that"). Sharing a term is not saying the thing.

**The rule now.** Code may only move a verdict down. The same case becomes `unverified`, keeps the site passage so a reader can judge it, and gives the reason `Your site does say "<term>", so we have not called this wrong.` Only the model can make a verdict `confirmed`, with a quote that survives the substring and support checks.

**Tests.** The old test "flips contradicted to confirmed when the site uses the claim's own terms" pinned the behaviour this change removes. It now expects `unverified`, the site passage and the reason. A new pair of fixtures rebuilds the stored report's two claims in neutral words (an invented business, the same site sentences), and both came out `confirmed` on the old code before the fix. Mutations: restoring `verdict = "confirmed"` turns 3 tests red; dropping the reason turns 3 red; removing the downgrade loop altogether (leaving `contradicted`) turns 3 red.

**Stored reports.** The stored rows keep the final verdict, not the model's, so a flip cannot be read off a row. It leaves a signature, though: a `confirmed` site quote that is exactly the ±80-character window around the first exact occurrence of a term in the stored crawl's site text. A read-only sweep of all 74 rows (8 carry accuracy assertions, 39 of those assertions `confirmed` with a quote) found that signature on 7 assertions in 4 rows, ours among them. Downgrading those 7 changes no headline and no fix on any of the 4 reports (`toReportView` → `headlineFinding` / `allFixes`, before and after); it changes only the "checked and fine" list. The per-report table is in the workspace decision file, since the rows name prospects.
