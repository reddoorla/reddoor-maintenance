# 2026-10-21 — migrations retry on SQLITE_BUSY across processes (#1674), and the never-measured list is sorted (#1677)

Landed in #1687 (this entry) and #1682. One worker session, started by the queue runner at about 4:30pm PT on the operator's pick.

**#1674.** #1672 fixed the `_migrations` marker race between clients in one process, and its review found that separate OS processes fail one step earlier, at the DDL itself. The new `tests/db/migrate-processes.test.ts` reproduced it exactly before the fix. Six `node --import tsx` children were held at a stdin barrier, then released together on a scratch `file:` database with the newest `ADD COLUMN` pending. In 3 of 3 rounds, 5 of 6 threw `SQLITE_BUSY: database is locked`. One round's error was a bare `Error: database is locked`, with no code and no prefix, and `isBusy` matches it only by message. The control was the same six on a fully migrated database, and it returned `[]` six times every round. That control is what makes the red worth believing. After the fix the test passed in every run. The reviewer also ran the harness separately against `origin/main`'s `migrate.ts` and got 5 of 6 again.

The fix moves `isBusy` and `retryOnBusy` unchanged from `prospect-audits.ts` to `src/db/busy.ts` and wraps all four of the runner's statements in the retry. Retrying a whole `executeMultiple` batch is safe only because of the current shape of `MIGRATIONS`. Every `ADD COLUMN` migration is a single statement, and every multi-statement migration is `IF [NOT] EXISTS` throughout. A batch of `ADD COLUMN; CREATE INDEX` would commit its first statement, retry, and swallow its own "duplicate column", leaving the index unmade. Nothing enforces that shape yet. The review filed it as #1690.
