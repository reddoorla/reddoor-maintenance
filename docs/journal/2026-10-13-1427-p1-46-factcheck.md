## 2026-10-13 — P1-46: our side fact-checked against FEMA, county records and the broker's originals; three flood sentences corrected (roalson-interests #300)

The operator asked for a fact-check of every place our packages contradicted what Matt Howard or the broker said, made against the broker's originals or public data, never against our own output. Three independent checks ran: flood, acreage and public facts. Each worked only from FEMA's live NFHL services, StratMap/CAD records, the original PDFs, TxDOT's roadway inventory, TDLR and the City of Boerne's Legistar.

**Where we were wrong:**

- **Menger Springs Road.** "Along the edge" came from a 422 sq ft sliver of approximate Zone A reaching at most 1.2 m into a 12.7-acre site, which is mapping noise. The broker's "no floodplain" was the fair reading.
- **St. Mary's.** The "along the edge" overlap came from the county parcel lines. The broker's own 2023 ALTA survey certifies Zone X, and his Land ID outline sits 0.5 m clear of the AE zone.
- **Walzem.** "5% floodway; 7% 1% annual chance" read as if the 100-year area were 7%. It is 12%, and 5% of that is floodway.

`flood_verdicts.py` changed in three ways. A share under 0.5% now counts as noise. The 1% annual chance (100-year) share is reported whole, with the floodway inside it. Percentages are whole numbers instead of 5% steps, which moves IH 35 from 45 to 47 and 25331 from 40 to 38. All five changed sentences equal the check's own figures, and the other 18 are unchanged. Our `acres()` read 0.33% low because it used metres-per-degree at the equator; only figures quoted in chat and journals used it.
