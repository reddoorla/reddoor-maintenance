# 2026-11-17 — P1-87 PR 4: the Lately feed, through four review rounds

Landed in [reddoorla/reddoor-maintenance#2070](https://github.com/reddoorla/reddoor-maintenance/pull/2070). Two sessions carried it: continuation 5 built it from a written plan with a Haiku implementer and ran round 1, and continuation 6 folded rounds 1 to 3 and ran rounds 2 to 4.

The overview's first screen gains its third panel, **Lately**: the newest 8 lines of the last 7 days, each a Pacific stamp, what happened, and the site. It draws only on what the snapshot already held, plus one query: recorded events (never `fleet_swept`), reports sent, leads delivered (a count and the site, never the sender), and the nightly. Sweeps are excluded in a second, feed-only read of `fleet_events`, because the shared read caps at 20 rows before filtering and three sweep rows a night can fill it; `/fleet`'s "Recently" fold keeps its read and still shows sweeps.

**Four rounds; rounds 1 to 3 dirty, round 4 clean.** Lenses were Opus; each round's fixes were applied by a Haiku fixer from a written brief (workspace `plans/2026-11-16-p1-87-overview-reorg/pr4-round{1,2,3}-fixes.md`).

- **Round 1 (`ea08043a`), 6 majors.** The plan gave the nightly a line per step and the feed a cap of 8. Nobody counted that 8 steps fill 8 lines: on any night the nightly ran, nothing older than its run could show. At Tue 11am PT, three Monday reports and a Monday vulnerability cleared were pushed out by five "ran to the end" lines.
  - A failed read became "Nothing recorded in the last 7 days." beside a tile saying "not readable".
  - Leads were grouped by "Oct 26" with no year, before the window filter, so leads a year apart counted together.
  - The same weekday twice in 7 days made "Mon 6:07pm" at the bottom look later than "Mon 5:47pm" at the top.
- **Round 2 (`591694da`), 4 majors.** Round 1's fix (a summary plus a line per step that did not finish) still filled the cap on a bad night: all 8 steps failed gave nine lines.
  - `align-self: start` on Lately only helps when Lately is the shorter panel. At 1280px a long feed grew the row from 498px to 829px, Health and Happening with it, and a CSS-text pin passed throughout.
  - The page could hide its lines when one source failed, and no test saw it.
  - A step's outcome was pinned only as "failed".
- **Round 3 (`7eb933ba`), 2 small majors.** A whitespace-only stored site name rendered as an empty site after " · ".
  - The new 1280px browser assertion ("every line's text starts at one x") measured nothing. Its dated row was the oldest of 7 seeded rows, so the 8-line cap cut it, and every stamp it compared was the same width.
- **Round 4 (`5e318e26`): clean on all three lenses, nits only.**

**The night is now exactly one line** (decision 4, the lead's pick under the brief's latitude on feed wording). It reads "Nightly run: 6 of 8 steps ran to the end (Backup: failed; Live forms: still running when the night was written)". It names at most two steps that did not finish, then "N more did not". Sketch 3 showed four step lines; a line per step would need a per-source cap.

**Beliefs corrected on contact.**

- A plan's cap must be counted against what can fill it. The 8-line cap and the line-per-step rule were each reasonable, and together they starved the feed.
- `align-self` on one grid item does not stop it from stretching its siblings; `align-items: start` on the grid does.
- A browser assertion over seeded rows must check that the row it depends on rendered. The cap can silently cut it.
- `?? []` on a failed read makes a page lie. Every source now carries its unreadable state to the page, which says "Could not read reports and leads just now." before its lines, in ordinary ink.
