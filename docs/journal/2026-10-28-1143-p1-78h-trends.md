**The defects, by kind.**

- **Zero against gap, through every path.** By round 5, every way a night can lack a number is a gap that gives its reason:
  - a night with no row;
  - an unread block;
  - a hand-written block that is not an object, or an array;
  - a negative or infinite count;
  - the night after a gap on a running total, which has nothing to subtract from;
  - a fall in a running total.

  Round 1 found a 0 drawn invisibly, like a gap, and a fall drawn below the axis. Round 4 found that `events: {value: 7}` read as an empty object, so the next night drew a false column of 2 sites cleared, and that a negative count drew as a 0 column.
