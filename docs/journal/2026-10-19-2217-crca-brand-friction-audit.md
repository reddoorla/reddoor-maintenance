## 2026-10-19 — CRCA Brand Friction Audit, run #1: the deck, a repeatable playbook, and three instruments that lied (#1424)

The operator asked for the first paid Brand Diagnosis ("BCF" turned out to be the Brand Credibility Framework, and the product is the Friction Audit) for CRCA, a Boise background-screening firm. All the work lives in `reddoorla/reddoor-workspace`: `audits/2026-10-19-crca-brand-friction-audit/` holds the evidence, findings and deck notes, and `playbooks/brand-friction-audit/` holds the recipe the operator asked for so the next prospect audit is machinery, not a one-off. This repo carries only Operator decisions 136 (#1424) and this entry. The deck is a Figma Slides copy of the BCF deck that contains the Rubrik example. It was reviewed by the operator and sent to Tim, who is expected to rework it.

**The brief cost an hour before any research started.** "BCF" existed only in a Figma slide footer. The client's legal name appeared only in a Harvest screenshot attached in Discord. The Rubrik example was the duplicated deck itself, not a separate file. And the claude.ai Figma connector is per session: it was "connected" but `enabledInChat: false` until the session restarted. The playbook's brief template now asks for all four up front.

**Three instruments gave confident wrong answers, and each was caught by a reading from a different authority.**

- **The broken form.** curl showed "Error: Contact form not found." on two service pages. A browser showed white text on a white card, so a visitor sees an empty box. Later the operator could not find how a visitor reaches those pages: the nav goes to `/services/#…` anchors, and only the homepage's two "Learn more" card buttons link to them. Walking the path in a browser settled it.
- **Search.** WebSearch said crcascreening.com appeared in 0 of 7 buyer searches, including the client's own name. The operator's Google shows it, and it "searches pretty well". WebSearch still returns roofing associations for "crca". A direct Google/Bing/DuckDuckGo check from the container failed its control (no results even for "checkr"), so the claim was withdrawn and the deck's Search column dropped. Rule written into the playbook: a person checks search claims on Google, or the client's Search Console is used.
- **The prospect audit.** Its DNS checks fail on every site from a cloud container, the reddoorla.com control included. Its Answers and Readability scores for Verified First (0 and 36) measured a cookie-consent wall, not the site. Its AI Visibility number survived both checks: CRCA 0 of 5 category searches, Verified First 2 of 5, Thuro 0 of 5. That became the deck's visibility finding.
- **PBSA held up.** "CRCA is not on the accredited list" was confirmed by name searches in the PBSA directory, with Verified First as the positive control, and CRCA's own questionnaire names accreditation as a goal.

**Fonts are a structural limit of the Figma connector.** It cannot load Helvetica Neue (a local Mac font) or Pragmatica (Adobe Fonts), so text in them cannot be edited from a session. Org-uploaded fonts would need the Figma Organization plan, a Helvetica license, and connector support that a March 2026 forum report says is missing. The operator chose Inter for editing, and the Figma connector's `use_figma` converted 13 text styles and 72 text boxes. The operator then wanted the house fonts for presenting, so the playbook now carries a small Figma development plugin (`figma-font-swap/`) that round-trips Inter ↔ Helvetica Neue / Pragmatica on a Mac. Its first real run worked. Pragmatica fell back to Helvetica on the operator's laptop because it is not activated there.

**Landing #1424 took about five hours of wall clock for a 20-line docs change.** Its checks passed on every head. It went BEHIND on every attempt as other sessions landed. Three other sessions took the next Operator decisions number first (133, 134, 135), so the item was renumbered three times. One pnpm registry-signature failure needed a re-run. The workspace notes now cite the item by title, not number. If many sessions file decisions at once, appending numbered items to one list is itself a source of conflict.
