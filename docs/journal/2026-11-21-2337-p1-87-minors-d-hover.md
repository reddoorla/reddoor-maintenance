**The preview is the chart's, not a pointer's.** All three hover fixes are one rule on the hit rect: an enter or a move from any non-touch pointer previews its night, and a night's leave ends the preview only if that night still holds it. #2171 needed the second half because the svg's own `pointerleave` never fires when the pointer drops from a column onto the chart's empty area below it. Ending any preview on a leave (mutant 2171-b) breaks the case review round 1 found: a pen leaving its night wiped the preview a mouse resting on another night had taken since. #2172's move restore skips while a key night is placed, so after a key step or a click the readout keeps following the key night until the pointer enters another (the round 3 I rule from PR 8a; mutant 2172-b).

**Belief corrected: letting a pen preview made M1 invisible to the #2175 test.** Once a pen's `pointerenter` previews (#2169), the pen's own enter moves the preview onto the tapped night before the click, so a click that sets the key night without ending the preview (M1) left the readout right by accident. The test now nudges the resting mouse 1 px on its own night first, which restores its preview (#2172's move), and then taps with the pen, so the click alone has to move the readout. M1 is red at the final commit, 1/156. This is the one existing test changed, and only in its set-up; every assertion it held still holds.

**#2164 took three rounds to get right.** The first build started the intro timer on the first `visibilitychange` to visible. Review round 1 found that this broke F2 (a refresh plays no intro on what it inserts): a tab shown with its view already due re-read at once, and the re-read's new rows played the intro. Round 2 found that a show-time check still let the clock's 60 s tick re-read inside the intro. The fix that held: while the intro is on, the cycle clock's re-read is held, not dropped, and runs once at the intro's end. The intro now plays whole on every path. **The cost:** a tab shown with stale data gets its new data up to `INTRO_MS` (2 s) later than before. The motion itself is unchanged: no colour, timing or copy changed.

**countUp started at mount, not on first frame.** In a hidden tab rAF does not run, so the count's clock started at mount and its first frame landed past `COUNT_MS`, ending the count instantly beside sparklines that animated in full. Two round-1 lenses found this independently. It now takes `start` from its first frame when mounted hidden (mutant 2164-c).

**The #2164 browser test proves the timer waits, not that the bars are held.** Playwright cannot hide a page, so an init script overrides `Document.prototype.visibilityState` and exposes a `__show()` that flips it and dispatches `visibilitychange`. A MutationObserver stamps when `data-motion-ready` appears and when `data-intro` goes. The control arm (loaded visible) had to pass first: its intro ended INTRO_MS after ready, within -20/+500 ms. The hidden arm's intro is still on at INTRO_MS + 500 ms, and ends INTRO_MS after the show within the same band.
