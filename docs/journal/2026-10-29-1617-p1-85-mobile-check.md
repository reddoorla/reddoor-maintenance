# 2026-10-29 — P1-85: the mobile check measures after styles apply, against the phone's width

Landed in reddoorla/reddoor-maintenance#1883 (fixes #1871). The cost decision is in the workspace, `decisions/2026-10-29-1615-p1-85-mobile-check-cost.md`.

The 10-29 nightly put LA Homelessness Initiative, MSOT and Reddoor at Broken for "pages fail to render on mobile", each on one route and one device. None of the three reproduced. LAHI `/`, MSOT `/` and Reddoor `/portfolio` read `scrollWidth` equal to the device width (412 or 390) at `domcontentloaded` and again at `load`, two reads each. They still fit with every `.css` response delayed 4 s. On these SvelteKit pages `domcontentloaded` waits for the module scripts, and those wait for the head stylesheets, so the pre-CSS race #1871 described could not be shown on them. An old-code fleet run at 8:33am PT flagged three _other_ routes: beachfront-dentistry `/your-first-visit`, revogen `/surgical-grafts/sports-medicine` and roalson `/properties/highway-77-at-general-cavazos-kingsville`. The old flags were noise. The nightly log could not say why any of them failed, because a pageerror, a missing `<main>`, a thrown evaluate and overflow all wrote the same `ok: false`.

**The belief corrected on contact.** #1871 said the iPhone 14 runs on WebKit and does not zoom. In `defaultBrowserRunner` both devices run on `chromium.launch()`; only the descriptor differs. Under that emulation `innerWidth` widens with the page up to 4× the device width: a local 600px block read `innerWidth` 608 on both devices, and a 2000px pre-CSS image read 1608 against a capped 1560 (390 × 4). The issue's 1648 is 412 × 4. So the old `scrollWidth > innerWidth + 2` could only fire on a page more than four times too wide, which is in practice the pre-CSS race. It was blind to every real overflow on record.

The fix extracts `probeMobile`. It waits for `load` (10 s cap), then for `document.fonts.ready` (10 s cap, skipped when `load` never fired). It compares `scrollWidth` with `descriptor.viewport.width`, keeping the 2px tolerance. And it puts the cause on the entry, which the offender string prints: `(overflow 449px > 412px)`, `(js error)`, `(no visible <main>)`, `(width not measured)` or `(load failed)`. A page with no viewport meta now fails at about 980px; review round 1 caught that it was unpinned. The three named mutations (no style wait, `innerWidth` again, tolerance 0) each turn the new real-Chromium tests red, 3 of 3.

**Cost, the brief's stop.** Fleet browser pass, no write-back, one run each from a cloud container: old 968 s, new 1197 s, **+229 s**, past the brief's ~2 min line. Split live on five routes, `load` adds 0.4–1.1 s per probe and fonts about 10 ms; no probe hit the cap. The cost is the wait itself, not hung pages. The operator chose "Land as is" (about 9:20am PT).

**What the new check flags.** CalTex `/` at 462px on the Pixel 7, intermittently (two reads of three; a `div.w-screen.p-8.bg-primary` banner ends at 437px), filed as reddoorla/caltex-landing#102. Vineyard `/contact` at 449 / 438px on every read, already reddoorla/vineyard-custom-homes#88. Beachfront `/team-members/stacey` and MSOT `/contact` fail on `(js error)`, Pixel 7 only, and Revogen `/surgical-grafts` on `(load failed)`. Those three are not overflow and were not investigated; P1-90 (week of 11-02) is the fan-out.
