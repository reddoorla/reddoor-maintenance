## 2026-10-13 — The archive line answers two questions, not one

The closing line asked one question with two answers in it. "Safe to
archive" required three things: the ask cleared on the live site, nothing
left only in the container, and no next step that belongs to this session.
The P1-46 session ended with "Not yet safe to archive: the 23 packages go
live only after Matt signs off … so archiving this one loses nothing." The
operator asked what that meant, and rightly: it said both no and yes. The
first condition failed and the other two held, so the line had no honest
single form.

The conditions answer different questions. Whether archiving loses anything
depends on the container and on who owns the next step. Whether the task is
done depends on what a visitor sees, and that often waits on people (a
client's sign-off, the operator's release go-ahead) no session can hurry.
Folding the second into the first meant every session whose work ends in
someone else's hands could never say "safe", which made the line worthless
as the thing the operator archives on.

`CLAUDE.md` now defines "safe to archive" as "archiving loses nothing" (the
container and next-step conditions) and makes "done" a separate, required
half of the line. There are three forms: "Safe to archive this session. The
task is done.", "Safe to archive this session. The task is not done: …"
(naming what a visitor still does not see, what it waits on, and where the
handoff is written down), and "Not yet safe to archive: …". The CalTex
lesson the old wording carried, a session calling itself done while its
content sat unpublished, is kept: "done" still means checked against the
live result, and the not-done form must say so out loud.
