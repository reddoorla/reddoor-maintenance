## 2026-10-19 — EOA site scope: a MemberClicks association, a phone layout that isn't one, and the RFP kit as price calibration (workspace `8560907`)

The operator asked for a scope of a Reddoor rebuild of eoa-assn.org (cockpit brief, 10-19). Mid-session he added the context that matters most: his father is part of EOA, and EOA is collecting bids. The deliverable is internal and lives in `reddoorla/reddoor-workspace` at `proposals/2026-10-19-eoa-site-scope/`, as a Markdown file, a 20-page PDF, `inventory.csv` and screenshots. This repo carries only this entry. Nothing was written to Turso, and nobody at EOA was contacted.

**Who and what.** The Eastern Orthopaedic Association is a society of orthopaedic surgeons covering 26 states and territories. Its site runs on MemberClicks (Joomla, template `buckhead-jt`, with `www` a CNAME to `cloudflare.memberclicks.net`). Its postal address is the same suite as Data Trace, which publishes its journal and hosts its abstracts on xCD, so Data Trace is very probably its management company and the real audience for a bid. `sitemap.xml` is an empty `<urlset>`, so the inventory came from a polite link crawl that honoured `Crawl-delay: 10`. It found 61 URLs, 56 distinct pages, 35 of them plain content. The remaining 21 are MemberClicks routes (10 gated video pages, 10 CME-record form wrappers, and login). There are 33 distinct MemberClicks forms and 38 PDFs. `/meeting-app` is linked from the site and answers 404.

**The instrument that nearly lied in this session was my own screenshot.** A 390 px Playwright context without `isMobile` produced a neatly responsive-looking home page. Lighthouse then failed the viewport audit. The page has no viewport meta tag at all, so on a real phone (Playwright's iPhone 13 profile) `innerWidth` is 980 and the desktop layout is scaled down. The Williamson Homes control gives 390 with `width=device-width`. The first screenshot was discarded. Its lesson: a viewport size is not a device, and a mobile claim needs device emulation plus a control that reads `innerWidth`.

**Controls.**

- **Lighthouse.** Run directly with maintenance's pinned 12.6.1. The first control, hedloc.net, gets a 502 from the egress proxy, so williamson-homes.com replaced it. It read desktop P94 A100 BP100 SEO92 against the P93/100/100/90 recorded that morning. EOA's mobile home page is P58 A55 BP68 SEO83, with LCP 8.4 s.
- **The prospect audit.** Run with `TURSO_DATABASE_URL` unset, plus `--out` and `--no-probes`; this session has no Anthropic key, so `analyze` and the answer-engine stages were not measured. Two stages failed the control:
  - The DNS stage reported no MX and no SPF for both EOA and Williamson. Google DNS shows MX records for both. This is the same failure the 2217 CRCA entry recorded: it happens from every cloud container.
  - The alt-text counter said 0 of 160 Williamson images have alt text, on a site Lighthouse scores 100 for accessibility.

  Neither stage is reported in the scope. The axe scan (9 rule failures on EOA, 0 on the control), the apex check (EOA's `https://eoa-assn.org` fails its certificate check, while the control redirects cleanly) and the findability and readability scores passed their controls.

- **A first-time fact.** The `prospect-audit` CLI already has a local path with no store: without Turso it needs `--out`, warns that the daily cap is not protecting the run, and writes nothing to the store.

**The RFP machinery, used late.** The operator asked "did you use the rfp machinery?" I had not. `rfp-analyze` is a laptop-only checkout with no `origin`, so it cannot run in the cloud. Its inputs and outputs live in Dropbox `/Marketing/RD_RFP_content`:

- two priced responses: Baxter State Park, $76–79k for about 45 pages over 20–22 weeks with $350/mo maintenance; and Incompass, $77k over 17 weeks at $150/hr and $200/hr for the creative director
- two rfp-analyze reports, Ohio Access to Justice and Dulverton, with their unit rates
- `04_copy_admin`, which is empty
- INTA, which turned out to be a brand RFP, not a website one, so it calibrates nothing for an AMS-fronted site

I checked the Baxter and Ohio figures against the files myself. The scope now carries an rfp-analyze-format estimate for the recommended option, done by hand: 157 / 198 / 255 production hours. With Baxter-style design phases that comes to about $51–65k, against $76–79k in Baxter's shape, and the operator prices it. I had first given the calendar as 6–10 weeks, from the Williamson builds. Reddoor's own bids promise 16–22 weeks because design phases run first, so the scope now says 16–20. **Belief corrected:** a build time and a bid timeline are different numbers, and the RFP kit, not the build journal, is where the bid one lives. The next scope of a bid should open the RFP kit first.

**Recommendation in the scope.** Option (a): a SvelteKit + Prismic site for the public pages in front of MemberClicks, deep-linking to `eoa.memberclicks.net`, which already serves EOA's forms and profile, so it keeps working when `www` moves. SSO through MemberClicks' OAuth/OIDC is a priced add-on. A full AMS migration is outside what Reddoor does, and a reskin inside MemberClicks is something MemberClicks sells itself. The open questions are led by "is there an RFP?" and "do we disclose your father's membership?".
