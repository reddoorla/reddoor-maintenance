## 2026-10-12 — Roalson's selected map pin is Erik's Ri-Pin2, and the other pins lose their stroke (roalson-interests#289, `c487c38`)

Erik asked in Discord #roalson-interests at 14:27Z for two changes: an SVG of his own for the selected pin, and no stroke around the secondary pins. The operator said yes to both. Erik's first export, `Ri-Pin.svg`, had a bump on its left side; his second, `Ri-Pin2.svg` (15:31Z), redraws the garnet ground as a circle plus a bottom. It went live at 16:18Z. The operator answered one design question on the way (Operator decisions 111, reddoor-maintenance#1313).

**The stroke was holding up the contrast, and the PR that added it said so.** #282 kept a solid garnet outline on the dimmed pins on purpose, and its contrast guard measured that outline. Remove it and the fill is the pin's only edge. At Erik's 50% fill the site's own gate fails on 356 of its ground pairs.

Measured on screen at 1440 with one sample per dimmed pin, the edge went from 10.25:1 (the stroke) to 2.76:1 on plain ground, and from 5.49:1 to 2.27:1 beside water. 0.81 is the lowest fill that passes. It is the clusters' value, set by the same binding pair, a white bridge deck over water. I proved the gate on known-good input first: it passes at 0.81 and fails at 0.80 on exactly that one pair.

The brief said not to quietly add the stroke back. So the question went to the operator through AskUserQuestion, with screenshots of both options: (a) 0.81 with no stroke, which passes AA; (b) 50%, which fails it; (c) 50% plus a soft shadow. He picked (a). Erik's "50% or less" from 10-09 is therefore overridden, and the PR's draft reply tells him so.

**Erik's path segments that looked stray were not.** The body path starts and ends with short segments of 0.3 to 0.8 units where it meets the circle. They looked like the anchor points that caused his first bump, so I trimmed them. A pixel diff at 5× then showed 793 changed edge pixels, with a maximum channel delta of 128. The control, the file against itself, read 0. So the paths ship verbatim. The only clean-up is the viewBox: his 792×612 artboard became the drawing's own bounding box, `240.3 110.5 311 390`. A browser `getBBox` confirmed its bottom centre is the tip.

**Size and anchor were kept by drawing inside the old box.** Ri-Pin2 sits in a nested `<svg>` at y 0.10 with height 0.801019, the comp glyph's own height, inside the unchanged 1 × 0.901019 pin box. `xMidYMid meet` centres it horizontally. So the box, the bottom-centre anchor, the 1.8× scale about the tip and the focus ring are all untouched. The new head is 0.639S across where the old one was 0.620S. The tip check uses a small path walker in the unit tests, plus a browser check that the drawn tip is within 0.5px of the anchor. Moving the box foot up 0.1S put the tip off by 8.64px, which is exactly 48 × 1.8 × 0.1.

**Review round 1 was clean of blockers.** It raised four optional points. The one worth its code was that the gate had lost its check on painted opacity when the old `@smoke` outline spec became a scaffold spec. With the pin's element dimmed as well, the pins would paint at 0.81² = 0.66, under 3:1, and the gate would stay green. An `@smoke` case now checks that a dimmed pin is painted at exactly its own `--map-dim`; with that mutation applied it reads 0.6561. Also from the review: the fill no longer eases on the two pins that swap active state, because the active pin is now a different drawing. That is cosmetic, and the CSS comment says so.
