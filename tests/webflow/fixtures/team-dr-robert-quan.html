Dr. Robert Quan

Dentist

Dr Robert Quan was born and raised in the Central Valley in Fresno, CA. He attended the University of the Pacific in Stockton, CA earning his degree in Biology. He earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at the University of the Pacific, Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry in San Francisco, CA.

Following graduation from Dental school, Dr Quan completed his residency at the Community Regional Medical Center and Veterans Affairs Hospital in Fresno where he continued to refine his dental abilities. After working in private practices in Fresno and Rochester, MN, Dr Quan is happy to call Southern California his new home while his wife completes her residency at UCLA in OB/GYN.

Dr. Quan loves that dentistry is a blend of function, technical skill, and artistic acuity. In his free time, he enjoys spending time with his wife, working out and playing volleyball.
